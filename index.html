To My Wife, My Home

“Of all the ways our story could have unfolded, I’m grateful it led me to you.”

My dearest Janvi,

When we first met on 31 January 2025, neither of us could have known exactly what all those small meetings, family conversations and traditions would grow into.

And then came 4 February — your first Instagram message. One simple message, and suddenly there was an “us” beginning quietly between all the wedding discussions.

Since then, we have collected so many little pieces of a life together — engagement memories, shopping, pre-wedding shoots, Mehendi, Mandvo, Haldi, Sangeet and finally our wedding day.

But my favourite part isn't only those big days. It is knowing that after all the celebrations ended, I got to come home with you.

Happy first anniversary, Janu. This first year was only the beginning.

Forever yours, Adi ❤️
